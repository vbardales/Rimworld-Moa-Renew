## Not applicable, with the reason

- Incompatibility pass: `About.xml` declares no `incompatibleWith`. DLC-absent pass: no DLC dependency and no patch guarded on a DLC.
- Nocturnal Animals: no patch (the emu is not named by that mod, so the moa stays diurnal by decision); there is nothing to assert.
- Settings, RIMMSQOL shortcut, restart test: the mod has no settings (`settings_audit: not_applicable`).
- Language switching and the game's reaction to a missing mod: the game's business, not tested.
- **S5, existing colony:** covered by `03` and `10`: `test-colony` is a save written without this mod, loaded with it active, which is what adding the mod to an existing colony is. A colony that already holds a moa is the save-and-reload scenario of `03`.
- **Forced, not waited:** the mating delay and the 7 days of incubation are the game's timers; `10` forces the hatcher's progress to its end and asserts what is born, not the duration.
- **Not played:** the dessicated emu texture (cosmetic, no assertion possible without a pixel check).

## Evidence: what to keep

Launch every request with `-EvidenceDir MoaRenew/Tests/Pickle/Evidence/<pass>`, a fresh folder each time (gitignored, on disk only).
Keep per pass: `summary.json`/`summary.md`, `junit.xml`, `messages.ndjson`, `Player.log`, `evidence-complete.txt` or `no-report.txt`, and
the `@review` capture opened and read (minified to JPEG). Never a whole `screenshots/` folder, never `report.html`. Read `exitReason`
first, then discovered against played. Keep the latest report per pass for the revision in the repository; delete the superseded one once
the newer exists; never delete what `STATUS.md` points to. History is one line per run in `docs/runs/`. Put the SHA in the request label:
the tree is staged when the ticket is played, not when it is filed.
